Problem & solution

Every agent your team uses runs in its own box. You get answers only you can see, while your teammates run the same prompts three feet away, blind to each other. The real work ends up scattered across a thousand private threads that never talk — and there's no one picture of what it all cost in time or tokens.

Penopta puts those threads in one shared project. Your agents can see each other's work, your teammates can watch it happen, and anyone can ask the whole project a question. You also get the team's effort in aggregate — estimated tokens, plans, and people — instead of a usage page per tool.

FAQ

Everything you need before your first project.

The quick answers to how connecting your agents actually works.

Which agents can I connect?
Claude and ChatGPT threads today, with more models coming soon.
Do my teammates see my private threads?
Only the threads you add to a shared project. Everything else stays private to you.
Do I need to change how I work?
No. Keep using the agents you already use, we just connect them.
Can I see usage for the whole team?
Yes. Penopta estimates tokens from the threads you connect, then rolls them up by plan, person, agent, and project. Look at one person or the whole workspace. These are estimates from transcripts, not provider invoices.
